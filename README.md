## What is HODL.DANCE?

**HODL.DANCE** is a decentralized, **creator-first memecoin launchpad** built on **BNB Smart Chain (BSC)**.

Every token starts its journey on a **Bonding Curve** — a transparent, automatic price discovery mechanism (CPMM). The price rises fairly with every purchase. 

Once a token reaches the **graduation threshold of 4.4 BNB**, liquidity is **automatically migrated** to **PancakeSwap V3** and the LP position is **permanently locked** inside the HODL.DANCE Locker smart contract.

From that point forward, **every single trade on PancakeSwap V3 generates real trading fees** that the original creator can claim anytime — for the entire lifetime of the project.

Unlike most launchpads that burn liquidity or unlock it after migration, **HODL.DANCE locks it forever**. Traders get guaranteed liquidity. Creators get guaranteed, ongoing revenue.

The platform also includes a **Rewards Program** (sharing platform fees with active participants), on-chain verification, on-chain advertising, Telegram buy bots, a full public API, and AI agent tooling.

## How It Works (Token Lifecycle)

1. **Launch**  
   Creator fills a simple form, uploads logo → TokenFactory deploys the token instantly.

2. **Bonding Curve Phase**  
   Token trades on a constant-product bonding curve. Price discovery is fully transparent and fair. 1% platform fee applies.

3. **Graduation (at 4.4 BNB)**  
   Automatic migration triggers:
   - Liquidity moves to PancakeSwap V3
   - LP position is **permanently locked** in the HODL.DANCE Locker
   - Any leftover tokens are **burned**

4. **Post-Graduation**  
   Token trades freely on PancakeSwap V3 with deep, locked liquidity.  
   Creator can claim accumulated V3 fees at any time via the platform.

5. **Earn for Life**  
   Real yield for creators. Guaranteed liquidity and exit opportunity for traders.

## Security & Design Principles

- No presale, no team token allocation, no hidden supply
- Leftover tokens from bonding curve **burned** at graduation
- LP position **permanently locked** — cannot be withdrawn by anyone
- Transparent **1% platform fee** on all bonding curve transactions
- On-chain verification, ads, and rewards where possible
- Core contracts undergoing / pending third-party audits
- All user interactions go through the verified contracts listed above

**DYOR**. Memecoins carry high risk. HODL.DANCE provides strong guarantees on liquidity and creator revenue mechanics.
